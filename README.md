# A Life with God | 與主同活

A bilingual English and Traditional Chinese static website for youth fellowship. It introduces a gentle daily rhythm of greeting God, worship, prayer, Scripture, and gratitude, with everyday prompts, a private check-in, and a small-group session outline.

## Use

Open [`index.html`](./index.html) in a browser. No installation, account, server, or external service is required. The language switch changes between English and Traditional Chinese.

Daily check-ins and the optional weekly note are stored in the browser's local storage on the current device only. They are not uploaded or shared. Avoid entering sensitive information, particularly on a shared device.

The page can be printed from the browser. Group leaders should adapt the suggestions to their church's teaching, safeguarding guidance, and the needs of their young people.
