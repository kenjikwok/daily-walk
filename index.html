NOT A PERFORMANCE PLAN不是表現清單

A rhythm, not a rulebook.建立節奏，而不是加添規條。

These practices are invitations to notice God and respond to love—not a scorecard for earning it. Start small, miss a day without shame, and return when you are ready.這些操練邀請我們留心神、回應祂的愛，並不是用來賺取祂的愛。由小處開始；即使有一天沒有做到，也不必自責，預備好時再重新開始。

“We love because he first loved us.” — 1 John 4:19「我們愛，因為神先愛我們。」——約翰一書 4:19

A SIMPLE DAILY RHYTHM簡單的每日節奏

Five small ways to turn toward God五個轉向神的小操練

There is no perfect order. Choose what fits your day.不必按固定次序，按當天情況選擇合適的操練。

01☀

Greet God向主問安

Before the day gets loud, pause. Remember you are God's beloved child, and offer the day to God.在一天忙起來以前，先停一停。記得自己是蒙愛的神兒女，把今天交託給神。

Try: “God, thank you for this new day. Be with me in it.”試試說：「神啊，感謝祢賜我新的一天，求祢與我同行。」

02♫

Worship唱詩敬拜

Listen to or sing a worship song. Notice a line that helps you remember who God is; you can pause, hum, or simply listen.聽一首或唱一首詩歌，留意一句幫助你記得神是誰的歌詞。你可以停下來、輕聲哼唱，或安靜聆聽。

Try: Keep one song for your walk, bus ride, or quiet moment.試試：預備一首詩歌，在散步、乘車或安靜時聆聽。

03✧

Pray honestly真誠禱告

Bring your real feelings, needs, and questions to God. Pray for your family, friends, church, school, and the people around you.把真實感受、需要和疑問帶到神面前，也為家人、朋友、教會、學校和身邊的人禱告。

Try: “Help me with ___; please care for ___; thank you for ___.”試試：「求祢幫助我面對___；求祢看顧___；感謝祢賜下___。」

04⌕

Read Scripture親近主的話

Read a short passage slowly. Ask what it shows you about God, what word stays with you, and how it might shape one choice today.慢慢讀一小段經文。想想它讓你看見神的甚麼、哪個字句留在心中，以及它如何影響今天的一個選擇。

Try: Read a Psalm, a Gospel story, or a few verses from your group's study.試試：讀一篇詩篇、一段福音書故事，或小組正在查考的幾節經文。

05♡

Give thanks獻上感恩

Notice a gift, a person, or a moment from the day. Thank God—and consider one small way to pass kindness on.留意今天的一份恩典、一個人或一個時刻。向神感恩，也想想可以怎樣把恩慈傳給別人。

Try: Name one thing you are grateful for before sleep.試試：睡前說出一件今天令你感恩的事。

FAITH IN REAL LIFE在日常生活中

Let ordinary moments become reminders讓平常時刻成為親近神的提醒

You do not need to stop everything for a long prayer. A breath, a sentence, or a moment of attention can help you turn toward God.不必每次都停下很久。一次深呼吸、一句短禱告，或片刻留心，都可以幫助你轉向神。

MORNING / 早上

When you wake or get ready: greet God and ask for help with the day.起床或準備出門時：向神問安，求祂幫助你面對今天。

ON THE WAY / 途中

Walking or riding the bus: listen to a song, pray for someone, or notice the world around you.散步或乘車時：聽詩歌、為一個人禱告，或留意身邊的世界。

SCHOOL / 學校

Before a class, test, or conversation: take one breath and ask for wisdom, courage, or kindness.上課、考試或談話前：深呼吸一次，求神賜智慧、勇氣或恩慈。

EVENING / 晚上

Before sleep: remember one good thing, bring one concern to God, and rest.睡前：回想一件美好的事，把一個掛慮交給神，然後安歇。

MAKE IT YOUR OWN按自己的步伐操練

A little space to practise today今天，留一點空間給操練

Choose what is possible. This is a gentle reminder, not a measure of your faith.選擇今天做得到的事。這是溫柔的提醒，不是衡量信心的標準。

FOR YOUTH FELLOWSHIP青年團契帶領參考

Try this together一起試試看

A flexible 40-minute gathering to introduce the rhythm. Adapt it to your group's size, culture, and time.一個約 40 分鐘的聚會流程，可按小組人數、文化和時間調整。

Create a safe space:建立安心的空間： Invite, don't pressure. Let people pass. Respect privacy, while explaining that leaders may need to seek help if someone is unsafe.以邀請代替勉強，容許組員選擇不分享。尊重私隱，同時說明若有人身處危險，帶領者可能需要尋求協助。

  1. 5 min

    Arrive and settle歡迎與安靜

    Welcome everyone. Take a quiet breath and name one word for how you arrive today. Passing is always okay.歡迎大家，安靜呼吸一次，並用一個字形容今天的心情。不想分享也完全可以。

  2. 8 min

    Explore the idea認識主題

    Read 1 John 4:19 together. Ask: what changes when daily practices are a response to love, not a way to earn love?一起讀約翰一書 4:19。討論：若每日操練是回應愛，而不是賺取愛，會帶來甚麼不同？

  3. 10 min

    Share a real-life moment連結真實生活

    Which moment in your week feels rushed, joyful, or difficult? What small reminder could help you turn toward God there?這星期哪個時刻令你忙亂、喜樂或感到困難？有甚麼小提醒能幫助你在那刻轉向神？

  4. 10 min

    Choose one practice選一個操練

    Invite each person to choose one doable practice for the coming week. Keep it personal, realistic, and free from comparison.邀請每位組員選一個下星期做得到的操練。讓計劃貼近個人、切實可行，也不互相比較。

  5. 7 min

    Pray and encourage禱告與彼此鼓勵

    Offer a short prayer for the group. Participants can pray aloud, silently, or simply listen.為小組作簡短禱告。組員可以開聲禱告、默禱，或安靜聆聽。

✦

WHEN YOU BEGIN AGAIN當你重新開始

Small, faithful moments add up.細小而真實的時刻，也能成為同行的路。

You are not walking alone. Keep showing up as you are, and let God's love meet you in the life you already have.你並不是孤單前行。帶著真實的自己繼續走，讓神的愛在你本來的生活中與你相遇。

“Draw near to God, and he will draw near to you.” — James 4:8「你們親近神，神就必親近你們。」——雅各書 4:8
